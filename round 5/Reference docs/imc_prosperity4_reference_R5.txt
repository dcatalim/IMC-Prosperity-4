`HYDROGEL_PACK` and `VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT` are standard delta-1 products. The 10 `VEV_*` products are **European call options** on `VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT`.

**Options time-to-expiry (TTE):**
- Options expire after 7 days total (1 day per round)
- TTE = 5 days at the start of Round 3's final simulation
- Historical data TTE alignment: Day 0 → TTE=8d, Day 1 → TTE=7d, Day 2 → TTE=6d

### Manual Challenge — "The Celestial Gardeners' Guild"

Trade with secret counterparties who each have a **reserve price** uniformly distributed between **670 and 920** at **increments of 5** (inclusive). You sell acquired Bio-Pods at a fair price of **920** after the round.

**Rules:**
- Submit **two bids** (b1, b2)
- If `b1 > reserve_price`: trade at b1
- If `b2 > reserve_price` AND `b2 > avg_b2` (mean of all players' second bids): trade at b2
- If `b2 > reserve_price` AND `b2 <= avg_b2`: trade at b2, but PnL is penalised by:

$$\left(\frac{920 - \text{avg\_b2}}{920 - b2}\right)^3$$

**Profit per unit = 920 − bid**. To maximise: set b1 high (captures low-reserve sellers cheaply), set b2 competitively relative to expected avg_b2.

Manual Challenge — "Vanilla Just Isn't Exotic Enough"
Trade AETHER_CRYSTAL (underlying) plus vanilla and exotic options. All products expire at end-of-round. You submit orders at t=0 and hold to expiry — no intra-round trading. This challenge is completely standalone (no relationship to Round 1 or algorithmic PnL).

Scoring: Average PnL across 100 simulations. PnL is marked to 'fair' value (average of 100 simulations) at expiry. Contract size = 3000 (PnL multiplier — applies uniformly to all products).

Underlying Simulation
AETHER_CRYSTAL follows Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM):

Drift: zero (risk-neutral)

Volatility: 251% annualized

Time grid: 4 steps per trading day, 252 trading days/year

Exotic Options Detail
Chooser Option:

Expires in 3 weeks

At the 2-week mark, the buyer selects whether it becomes a call or put — whichever is currently in-the-money

For the final week, it behaves as a standard vanilla option

Value at choice date: max(C(S, K, T=1W), P(S, K, T=1W))

Binary Put Option:

All-or-nothing payoff

Pays a fixed specified amount if S < K at expiry

Expires worthless if S ≥ K at expiry

Value: payout × N(-d2) under BSM (risk-neutral drift = 0)

Knock-Out Put Option:

Standard put payoff unless the barrier is breached

If the underlying trades below the knock-out barrier at any discrete grid point before expiry → option immediately becomes worthless

Barrier is checked discretely (not continuously); only grid points matter

Risk Management Notes
Unhedged exposure can lead to large losses — manage delta and vega across positions

The price column in the order submission interface is cosmetic only — ignore it for trading decisions

You may buy or sell up to the displayed volume in each product

PnL = (fair value at expiry − entry price) × quantity × contract size (3000)

Algorithm Architecture
Simulation Mechanics
run() is called once per iteration (1,000 iterations during testing, 10,000 for final scoring)

Each iteration receives a TradingState snapshot of the market

Orders sent that cross the book execute immediately (no latency)

Orders that don't fill are visible to bots; if no bot trades on them, they are cancelled at end of iteration

State is stateless between iterations (AWS Lambda) — use traderData string to persist data (max 50,000 chars, use jsonpickle)

Order Matching Rules
Price-time priority: best price filled first; ties broken by order age (oldest first)

A BUY order fills against SELL orders at or below its price (executes at the SELL order's price)

A SELL order fills against BUY orders at or above its price (executes at the BUY order's price)

Partial fills leave a resting order; any unfilled resting player order is cancelled at end of iteration

Options Pricing Context (VEV Vouchers)
The 10 VEV_* contracts are European call options on VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT. Key inputs for Black-Scholes or binomial pricing:

Parameter	Source
Underlying price (S)	Mid-price of VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT order book
Strike (K)	Encoded in symbol name (e.g. VEV_5000 → K=5000)
Time to expiry (T)	5 days in Round 3; 4 days in Round 4; decreases by 1 each round
Risk-free rate (r)	Assume 0 or calibrate from data
Volatility (σ)	Estimate from historical VELVETFRUIT_EXTRACT price data
Delta of each option changes as S, T, and σ change. Track your net delta across all positions (underlying + all vouchers) to manage directional risk.

Options Pricing Context (AETHER_CRYSTAL — Round 4 Manual)
All Round 4 manual products are written on AETHER_CRYSTAL with GBM dynamics (σ=251%, r=0). Use risk-neutral pricing:

Greek	Relevance
Delta	Directional exposure from underlying and vanilla options; hedge with AETHER_CRYSTAL
Vega	All long options are long vega at σ=251%; consider net vega across portfolio
Theta	Decay cost; shorter-dated vanillas decay faster
Chooser	Long vega play; value ≈ call + put − min(call, put) at choice date
Binary Put	Negative delta near strike; no vega above strike
Knock-Out Put	Lower premium than vanilla; loses value as S approaches barrier
BSM formulas (r=0 drift):

d1 = [ln(S/K) + 0.5·σ²·T] / (σ·√T)

d2 = d1 − σ·√T

Call = S·N(d1) − K·N(d2)

Put = K·N(−d2) − S·N(−d1)

Binary Put value = N(−d2) × payout

Chooser value at t=0 = Call(T=3W) + Put(T=2W) [approximation using put-call parity at choice date]

class Trader:

Key implication: fee doubles in cost for every √2 × increase in volume per product. Concentrating 100,000 into one product costs the same total fee (10,000) as splitting into ten 10,000-unit positions (10 × 100 = 1,000). Always spread budget — concentration is quadratically punished.

Total fee = Σ (volume_i / 1000)² across all products you trade.

Net PnL = Σ [volume_i × expected_return_i] − Σ [(volume_i / 1000)²]

The more you concentrate in a single good, the more expensive it becomes. Diversify to minimize fee drag.
