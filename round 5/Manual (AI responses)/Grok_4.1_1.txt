Core Mechanics (from the reference)

Budget: 1,000,000 XIRECs (allocate as percentages summing to ≤ 100%).
Fee: Quadratic penalty per product — fee = (volume/100) * (volume/100) ** budget (or similar quadratic form; concentration is heavily penalized).
Investment per good = (percentage / 100) × total budget.
PnL comes from price movement after news impact × your net position (long or short), minus fees.
You submit buy/sell direction + allocation % for each of the 9 goods. Unused budget expires worthless.
Goal: Maximize expected net PnL after fees across 100+ hidden simulations (average score).

The fee structure makes it a portfolio optimization problem with convex costs: spread capital across high-conviction signals rather than going all-in on one or two. Diversification reduces fee drag significantly.
