Challenge recap
In the Round 5 manual challenge you get a 1,000,000 XIREC budget, one day on the Ignith exchange, and exactly nine tradable Ignith goods tied to specific Ashflow Alpha news headlines. You can go long or short each product, but any unused budget simply disappears and does not add to profit, so you want to deploy essentially all of it.

Transaction costs are nonlinear: the fee per product scales more than linearly with the amount you put into that single product, so concentrating a huge fraction of the budget into one name is heavily penalized compared with spreading size across several high‑conviction ideas. Bearish positions consume budget just like bullish ones, so you should only short when the negative news is strong and fundamental, not just sentiment‑driven.

Interpreting the news signals
The reference explicitly tells you which signals are structurally strong versus narrative‑only. The “strongest fundamental signals” are Thermalite Cores (structural demand surge), Sulfur Ltd. / Sulfur Reactor (index inclusion and forced buying), and Pyroflex Cells (tax policy shock as a structural headwind). The “weakest/riskiest” are Scoria Paste (influencer narrative), Volcanic Incense (momentum only with a celebrity timing overlay), and similar hype‑driven stories.

There are also classic “disaster” headlines: production halts, health investigations, and scandals, which are much more credible short candidates than pure sentiment wobbles. You want to be net long the clearest structural winners, and selectively short the clearest structural losers, while staying small in pure hype names where the payoff is path‑dependent and fragile.

Position sizing and fees
The fee structure is designed so that “the more you concentrate in a single good, the more expensive it becomes,” and “fee scales quadratically with volume per product – doubling volume more than doubles the fee.” That means:

Spreading the same total budget across several products reduces total fees versus putting it all into one.

Adding a bit more size on your biggest position hurts more (marginal fee is high) than adding the same size to a smaller position.

Optimal allocations have multiple mid‑sized positions in your highest‑conviction ideas, with no single product dominating.

Tactically, this implies you should cap any single position at perhaps 20–25% of the budget, and keep most between roughly 5–20%, unless you have extreme conviction that the scoring engine massively rewards one scenario. You also generally want to use close to 100% of your budget, because unspent budget is worthless in the scoring and you are graded on net PnL after fees, not on risk‑adjusted return.

How to tweak if you disagree with a story
If you think influencer narratives are more powerful than the reference implies, you could shift a few percent from Lava Fountain Pen or Sulfur into Volcanic Incense and Scoria Paste (for example, +3% Volcanic, +2% Scoria, −3% Sulfur, −2% Lava Pen).

If you are more risk‑averse, cut Volcanic and Scoria down to 5% and 3% respectively, and reallocate that into Thermalite and Sulfur to keep fees low while leaning into fundamentals.

If you prefer more short exposure, slightly increase Pyroflex and Lava Cakes by a couple of percentage points each, taking from Volcanic/Scoria rather than from your highest‑quality longs.
